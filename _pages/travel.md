If you're joining us from out of town, you've got plenty of choices for lodging. As luck would have it (and because Moira needed another reason to rant about Bill Belichick), UNC has a home football game against Syracuse on October 24, so **we recommend considering a place to stay early.**  
\\
We will have a block of rooms at the Tru by Hilton in Chapel Hill, but there are other options to consider. There are also, of course, Airbnb rentals throughout the Triangle.

# Tru by Hilton, Chapel Hill 
We have a block of rooms with a group rate - we'll have more info shortly!  
1742 Fordham Blvd  
Chapel Hill, NC 27514

# Hampton Inn, Carrboro
Conveniently situated steps away from Friday evening's activities.  
370 E Main St Unit 100  
Carrboro, NC 27510

# The Carolina Inn, Chapel Hill
If you want to experience a Chapel Hill institution, [The Carolina Inn](https://www.carolinainn.com/) sits right on the edge of campus and puts you very close to Franklin Street.  
211 Pittsboro St  
Chapel Hill, NC 27516

# Graduate by Hilton, Chapel Hill
Right on Franklin Street! Next door to [Perennial](https://www.perennial.cafe/) coffee and across the street from the Gary's fancy cocktails at [The Crunkleton](https://thecrunkleton.com/).  
311 W Franklin St  
Chapel Hill, NC 27516
